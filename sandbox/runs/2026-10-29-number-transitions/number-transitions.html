Price update variations

Three ways to show that a live figure changed, and a load test for pages with many of them. Every card gets the same ticks, so you can compare them side by side. The digits that changed are the ones that react; the rest of the figure stays still. Changes and percentages next to the price just update, as on TradingView.

Where these come from

  • A: TradingView symbol page, measured live: the changed digits get a growing/falling class for 750 ms, colour only, removed by a timer.
  • B: close to react-stock-ticker (MIT), colour on change with a transition back.
  • G: A plus a short slide, made for this comparison.
  • Left out: rolling digits (NumberFlow, @sfinterface/numbers) render a strip of digits per column, which is what made the market overview slow; and AG Grid's cell flash and delta label.
