Opus gets 3: every invented piece is a documented stand-in, or an undocumented one it disclosed. Sonnet gets 2: it ignored the period-selector stand-in's content, used Dropdown where Select fits, and left out the Toggle.

### Rule compliance

- Hex, palette colors, `dark:`, `duration-`/`ease-`, `outline-none`, `transition-all`, inline styles, shadows and gradients: **none in either file** (grep). The only ramp classes are the chart `text-/stroke-utility-*` ones, which charts.md documents.
- Both models reach into Table internals to right-align numeric headers. Opus uses `*:justify-end` and Sonnet `[&>div]:justify-end`. Both disclosed it. It's forced by the library (no `align` prop).
- Both import the `Key` type unprefixed. Opus takes it from `react-aria-components` (`:27`), Sonnet from `react-aria`. This is minor, but the letter of the aria-prefix rule is broken.
- One primary per section: both models have the header's "Kjøp"/"Logg inn", the TV card's primary button and the EmptyState's "Logg inn". It's acceptable per section, and Sonnet admitted "Se live" could be secondary.
- Both score 2: clean on tokens, with the same small forced bends.

Both feel recognisably Finansavisen in their content: Norwegian copy, real tickers, NOK formats. Opus is closer in the index panel and the real-time switch. Sonnet's real-time logic breaks the brief's constraint that "data is delayed unless real-time is switched on". Both score 2, with Opus at the higher end.

### Visual quality

- Opus gets 2. The page is tidy in both themes, but at 375 the % change in Vinnere (the most important number) is clipped, three selectors scroll invisibly, and a few cards have holes.
- Sonnet gets 1. Four ⅓-card tables clip columns **on desktop**: Hot stocks hides its % change, and three tables hide their action column. The mobile chart has no y-axis. The Indekser card is sparse.

### Accessibility

- **Opus gets 3.** It has `aria-labelledby` sections, a labelled ButtonGroup and Table, period items named "1D, −0,04 %", a text summary of the chart, `aria-live` on the news list, an aria-labelled Select, and signs on every change value. The ticker link has the label "Gå til TECH".
- **Sonnet gets 2.** Labels and signs are right, and the star buttons have tooltips. But below `md` the stock name is `display:none`, so rows announce only "TECH". Periods have no change in their names. The contradictory Live/Forsinket states mislead every user.
- Neither model makes the whole row a link (`Table.Row href`). Opus says it did, in note 3.

### Honesty of notes

- **Opus gets 3.** It says plainly that it didn't look at the result. It lists each stand-in, the bent rules, 8 clashes and real library bugs (Table.Head padding, which I verified at `table.tsx:180` against `:274`). It didn't foresee the Vinnere clipping. It claims `Table.Row href` but doesn't use it, which is minor.
- **Sonnet gets 2.** It admits the result wasn't viewed and gives good gap analysis. It doesn't mention the hard-coded "Forsinket 15 min" contradiction. It says hiding columns makes the tables fit, which the render disproves. It frames real-time-equals-logged-in as an "assumption" and doesn't flag it against the brief.
