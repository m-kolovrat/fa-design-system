# Børs (market overview)

**Purpose.** Answer "what is happening on the market today?" in seconds, and lead into any stock.

**Audience.** Active private investors checking several times per trading day, mostly on a phone.

**Content and data.**

- Hovedindeksen Oslo Børs 2 124,29, −0,04 %, live 14:10, development over 1D–5Y; also Sverige, Danmark, USA.
- OBX, Dow Jones, S&P 500, NASDAQ, Brent, gull, EURNOK, USDNOK, Bitcoin, US 10Y, VIX.
- Vinnere and Tapere (Techstep +15,62 %, Proximar Seafood −89,19 %).
- Live Børsmorgen TV.
- The user's watchlist: porteføljeverdi and today's change.
- Børsnyheter by source: FA, TDN, Børsmeldinger, Pressemeldinger, Min watchlist.
- Mest omsatte, Hot stocks (unusual turnover), Mest shortet (andel, verdi, days-to-cover), new all-time highs and lows.
- Reports, earnings calls, webcasts; Sektorer; Kommende utbytter; Finanskalender.

**User actions.** Search a ticker, switch market and period, open a stock, add to or switch watchlist, filter news, play a webcast, download a report.

**Constraints.** Mobile-first, number-dense, Norwegian number formats. Rises and falls must be unambiguous through signs, not visuals alone. Data is delayed unless real-time is switched on. Logged-out users have no watchlist.
