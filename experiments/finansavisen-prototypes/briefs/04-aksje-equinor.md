# Aksjeside: Equinor (EQNR)

**Purpose.** Everything needed to decide whether to buy, hold or sell Equinor, without leaving Finansavisen.

**Audience.** Private investors who own or watch the stock, checking during the trading day and around reports, mostly on a phone.

**Content and data.**
- EQNR, Oslo Børs: 444,30 NOK, −6,60 (−1,46 %), live 14:12; development 1D, 5D, 6M, YTD, 1Y, 5Y, maks.
- Åpne 448,40, høy 450,70, lav 440,10, kjøp/salg 444,30/444,40, volum 1,08 M, omsetning 480,77 M, P/E 12,59, neste rapport 28.10.
- News and stock exchange notices (FA, TDN, Børsmeldinger, Pressemeldinger).
- Analyst consensus, recommendations (median kursmål 393,53), technical signals (SMA, RSI, MACD: kjøp/selg).
- Revenue and profit per year or quarter, order depth, key figures (direkteavkastning 3,39 %), latest trades, dividends, financial calendar, reports, earnings calls.
- Further areas: Forum, Regnskap, Profil, Bjellesauer, Topp 50 aksjonærer, Historikk, Teknisk analyse.

**User actions.** Add to or remove from watchlists, change period, switch annual/quarterly, filter news, download a report, add an event to the calendar, play a call.

**Constraints.** Mobile-first. Current price must always be at hand. Data is delayed unless real-time is on; some analysis is subscriber-only.
