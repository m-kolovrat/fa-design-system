# Pluss-artikkel (subscriber article)

**Purpose.** Deliver a paid market story worth paying for, with live prices alongside so readers can judge the trade themselves.

**Audience.** Active private investors and traders reading on a phone, often during market hours.

**Content and data.**

- Kicker "Andreas Wold Hofstad (31):", headline "Shorter shippingaksje for 25 millioner", two-sentence ingress.
- Section (Shipping), "Publisert 11:39", journalist (Eivind Aakre) with a way to send tips.
- Photo with caption and credit; body with subheadings and a quote («Ikke opplevd før»).
- Live price for each company mentioned: Bruton (BRUT) 57,00, −1,04 %, returns 1D–5Y; International Seaways (INSW.US) 113,70, +2,64 %.
- Investor profile: beholdning 22,06 M NOK, 6 aksjer, største investering LUMI, kjøpt 41,39 M / solgt 25,31 M siste 12 mnd.
- Related story, AI-use note, comments, further reading.

**User actions.** Follow the person, companies, section or journalist in MittFa; open a stock or investor profile; change return period; share; comment.

**Constraints.** Mobile-first. Non-subscribers see headline, ingress and opening only, then an offer to subscribe or log in. Ads must not interrupt reading.
