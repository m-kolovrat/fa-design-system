# Forside (front page)

**Purpose.** Where investors start their day: what moves the market now, and what the newsroom thinks matters.

**Audience.** Retail investors, finance professionals and business leaders, visiting several times a day, mostly on a phone.

**Content and data.**
- Market snapshot: Oslo Børs 2 126,86 (+0,09 %), Brent 114,93 USD (−0,20 %), Nikkei 225, Bitcoin, EUR/NOK 10,40, USD/NOK 9,32, gull, US 10 år.
- The newsroom's chosen stories. Each has a headline and may have a kicker ("Fredrik Dyve forhandlet i måneder:"), an image, and a marker: Siste, Populært, Mye lest, Børsoppdatering, Pluss or Annonse.
- Latest Børsoppdatering ("Rally i rigg og oljemygg – full kollaps i sjømat").
- Mest lest, commentary (Trygve Hegnar), video (Børsmorgen), Kapital, Motor, and job ads (Karriere).

**User actions.** Open a story, search a ticker or ask Investorchat, follow a topic, play video, reach Børs, Watchlist, Forum and Bjellesauer, get the app.

**Constraints.** Mobile-first. Logged-in readers are greeted by name; logged-out readers are invited to create a free account. Pluss stories are recognisable before opening. Sponsored content is clearly separate from journalism.
